Local AI

I run a fully offline AI system on one Mac: 100+ scheduled agents, a multi-model council and a voice assistant, at zero cloud cost. These pieces are open source. Write-ups of the bugs behind them, and the system running live, are on navii-cc.pages.dev.

Voice assistant

jarvis

Voice-first Mac assistant that keeps working offline: Ollama brain, local Whisper wake word, Piper British voice, and Mac control.

 LLM reliability

ollama-council

Two local models cross-check each other and a third breaks ties. Agreement is judged by meaning, with a FIFO queue and secret redaction.

 Grounding

triple-check

Stops local LLMs from making up numbers. Python does the math, two experts explain it, and a different model verifies every figure.

 macOS tooling

launchd-doctor

Finds and fixes the LaunchAgent bugs that fail silently, and pauses agents that keep failing with a circuit breaker.
